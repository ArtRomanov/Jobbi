Legal Counsel (Product & Privacy)

VIZOR APPS LIMITED , Posted 1 week ago

Apply

Senior

Full time

Remote

Premium

Vizor Games is hiring a dedicated in-house Privacy & Product Counsel to own privacy, product compliance and regulatory matters across Vizor’s games. You will be the primary legal point of contact for our product, game design, monetisation, marketing and data teams, helping them ship fast without creating regulatory debt and contributing directly to the growth of our games. This is a hands-on role with real ownership. You will be part of Vizor’s in-house legal team and partner with cross-functional teams across the company

For which tasks (responsibilities)?

Product & Regulatory Compliance

– Partner with product teams: work side by side with game design, monetisation and LiveOps from idea to launch, turning legal requirements into practical, player-friendly solutions

– Monetization: guide in-game offers, virtual currencies and other mechanics through consumer law in our key markets

– Marketing and platforms: review campaigns and creatives, and keep us on good terms with app stores and platform policies

– IP: clear product names and in-game content at an early stage, manage our IP portfolio and protect our brands

– AI: shape the rules for using AI tools in game development and in player-facing features

 – Own the player-facing terms: keep our Terms of Service, EULA and other player policies clear, fair and up to date

– Regulatory monitoring: track changes in consumer protection, gaming and online safety rules, explain what they mean for us, and work with external counsel where needed Proactively identify legal and regulatory hurdles, and design pragmatic solutions that balance business objectives with risk mitigation

Privacy & Data Protection

– Maintain the framework: keep and Vizor's global privacy and data protection program, policies and procedures

– Privacy monitoring: track and advise on privacy and data protection risks and requirements across digital markets and platforms affecting the mobile gaming sector

– Vendors & Partners: draft and negotiate data processing agreements and manage privacy obligations with vendors, partners and platform providers

– Minors' safety: advise on age-appropriate privacy safeguards in line with applicable law and platform requirements

– Requests and incidents: manage data subject requests and oversee the company’s response to personal data breaches and incidents

What kind of professional are we looking for?

– A law degree and a strong academic record

– 5+ years' PQE, in-house or at a law firm with a strong preference for experience in an international environment

– Hands-on experience in tech law: data protection, consumer protection, platform regulation and IP. Video game industry expertise is a plus

– Pragmatic, business-minded judgement and comfort translating legal risks into practical business advice

– Full ownership of your work: you prioritise a busy queue and see tasks through from the first question to the final outcome

– Collaborative way of working: you share context and progress openly, ask for input early, delegate where it makes sense and are comfortable giving and receiving feedback

– Strong written and verbal communication skills in English, coupled with strong interpersonal abilities
